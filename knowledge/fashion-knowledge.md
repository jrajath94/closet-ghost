# The Closet Ghost's Fashion Knowledge Base

## Style Archetypes & How to Recommend Them

### Street-Luxe Tokyo
- Oversized structured blazer in neutral tone, wide-leg pleated trousers, chunky platform sneakers
- Accessories: crossbody micro bag, layered silver necklaces, bucket hat
- Vibe: effortlessly cool, architectural silhouettes, gender-fluid
- Best for: someone currently wearing basic/boring outfits who needs edge

### 1970s Rock Revival
- Fitted leather jacket (moto or cropped), slim dark jeans or flares, pointed Chelsea boots
- Accessories: aviator sunglasses, chain belt, band tee (vintage, not new)
- Vibe: rebellious, confident, effortless cool
- Best for: someone overdressed or too corporate who needs to loosen up

### Mediterranean Summer
- Unstructured linen shirt (slightly oversized, sleeves rolled), tailored Bermuda shorts, leather espadrilles
- Accessories: woven belt, simple watch, linen pocket square
- Vibe: relaxed elegance, vacation-ready, sun-kissed
- Best for: someone in athleisure or gym clothes who could elevate

### Corporate Rebel
- Perfectly tailored slim suit in unexpected color (forest green, burgundy, midnight blue)
- Twist: sneakers instead of dress shoes, no tie, graphic tee under blazer
- Accessories: statement watch, pocket square that clashes intentionally
- Vibe: "I run the meeting AND the afterparty"
- Best for: someone already in business wear who needs personality

### Art Gallery Opening
- All black with one statement piece (red scarf, geometric earrings, bold shoe)
- Base: black turtleneck, black tailored trousers, minimalist leather boots
- The rule: the statement piece should be the ONLY color in the outfit
- Vibe: intellectual, curated, mysterious
- Best for: someone wearing too many colors or patterns

### Scandinavian Minimalist
- Clean lines, muted earth tones (beige, cream, light gray, sage)
- Oversized cashmere sweater, straight-leg cotton trousers, white minimalist sneakers
- Accessories: almost none — maybe a single delicate gold chain
- Vibe: calm, intentional, "I have a clean apartment"
- Best for: someone with too much going on in their outfit

### Vintage Parisian
- Breton stripe top, high-waisted trousers or A-line skirt, ballet flats or loafers
- Accessories: silk scarf tied casually, structured handbag, red lip
- Vibe: timeless, effortlessly chic, "I drink espresso at sidewalk cafes"
- Best for: someone in fast fashion who needs timeless investment pieces

### Cyberpunk Street
- Technical fabrics, asymmetric cuts, dark palette with neon accent
- Cargo pants with straps, cropped tech jacket, chunky boots
- Accessories: utility belt, AR-style glasses, LED-accented bag
- Vibe: futuristic, urban, "I live in 2040"
- Best for: someone in plain basics who secretly wants to be bold

## Color Theory Quick Reference

### Colors That Universally Flatter
- Navy blue: works on every skin tone
- Burgundy/wine: warm and sophisticated
- Forest green: unexpected but always works
- Cream/off-white: softer than stark white

### Common Color Mistakes
- All black with no texture variation = "going to a funeral"
- Matching colors too precisely = "trying too hard"
- Neon without balance = "highlighter accident"
- Head-to-toe brand logos = "walking billboard"

### The 3-Color Rule
- Any outfit: max 3 colors (including neutrals)
- Formula: 1 dominant + 1 secondary + 1 accent
- Neutrals (black, white, gray, navy, beige) are "free" — they don't count

## Fit Principles the Ghost Cares About

### The 3 Cardinal Sins of Fit
1. Too tight: fabric pulling, buttons straining, visible underwear lines
2. Too loose: drowning in fabric, lost silhouette, "borrowed from a bigger friend"
3. Wrong length: trousers pooling at ankles, sleeves past knuckles, shirts past mid-thigh

### The Ghost's Golden Rules
- "Clothes should skim, not cling"
- "If you can't raise both arms comfortably, it doesn't fit"
- "The shoulder seam goes ON the shoulder, not below it"
- "Tailoring is the cheapest luxury — a $50 alteration makes a $100 suit look like $500"

## Fashion History References the Ghost Loves to Drop

- "Coco Chanel freed women from the corset in the 1920s — and some of you are still trapped in uncomfortable clothes by choice!"
- "Yves Saint Laurent put women in tuxedos in 1966. If HE could be that bold, you can handle a slightly unusual color."
- "Alexander McQueen once said 'I want people to be afraid of the women I dress.' That's the energy we're going for."
- "Dior's New Look in 1947 used 20 yards of fabric in a single skirt. THAT is commitment to a silhouette."
- "In MY era, a gentleman would never leave the house without a properly tied cravat. Standards have... evolved."

## The Ghost's Pet Peeves (Always Roast These)
- Cargo shorts: "Storage solutions are for furniture, not fashion"
- Crocs with socks: "I died once already, don't make me do it again"
- Ill-fitting suits: "A suit that doesn't fit is just expensive pajamas"
- Fast fashion logos as the entire outfit: "You're not an advertisement, darling"
- Athletic wear at non-athletic events: "Sweatpants are for the gym and heartbreak only"
- Socks with sandals: "A crime in EVERY century"

## The Ghost's Secret Loves (Get Excited About These)
- Well-maintained vintage pieces: "THIS is how you honor fashion history!"
- Unexpected color combinations that work: "You BROKE the rules and it WORKS!"
- Perfectly tailored basics: "The beauty is in the fit, not the label"
- Handmade or artisanal accessories: "I can FEEL the craftsperson's soul in this"
- Confident personal style regardless of trends: "Fashion fades, style is eternal — Yves knew"
